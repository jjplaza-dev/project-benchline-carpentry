# ASSET_MANIFEST.md: Benchline Carpentry & Home Repair

Every image in the Phase 2 prototype is an **original SVG placeholder** drawn for this project (flat linework with measurement dimension lines). No photograph, stock image or third-party artwork has been downloaded, licensed or verified. Nothing here has been checked for permissions.

Real photography must be supplied by the owner. The placeholder files live under `public/assets/`; `src/data/assets.ts` holds each asset's id, path and alt text, and is the one place to update when a photo replaces a placeholder (set `kind: "photo"`, `isPlaceholder: false`, and update `path` and `alt`).

## Rules for every replacement photo

- **Sourcing:** the owner's own photographs of real Benchline projects are strongly preferred. Photograph in daylight, keep the camera level, and avoid people, license plates, house numbers and other identifying details.
- **Permission:** get the homeowner's permission before publishing any project photo.
- **License and attribution:** if anyone other than the owner took a photo, or any image is not the owner's own work, confirm in writing that it may be used on the site and note any required credit here. Do not use images pulled from search results or social media.
- **Alt text:** replace each placeholder's alt text with a short description of what the real photo shows.
- **Before and after pairs:** shoot the "before" and "after" from the **same camera position and framing** so the comparison slider lines up.
- **Format:** JPG or WebP, exported near the display size noted below. Provide width and height; the build uses aspect-ratio frames, so the listed ratio matters more than exact pixels.
